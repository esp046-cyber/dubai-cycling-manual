Heat illness

Stop, move to shade, sip water with electrolytes, cool neck and head. Confusion or collapse is an emergency: call 998.

Crash

Get out of traffic if you can, check for injuries, call 999 or 998, and do not move someone with possible neck or back injury unless there is danger.

Puncture

Repair in shade, not on hot asphalt. Check the tyre for thorns or glass before fitting the new tube.

Night riding

Use a strong front light and a red rear light, wear reflective gear, slow down, and avoid unlit isolated roads.

Trucks near JAFZA

Never ride beside a turning truck. Stay behind, where the driver can see you, and wait for it to clear junctions.

Sand and dust

Avoid braking hard on sand-covered edges. Slow down, protect eyes and mouth, and skip rides on very dusty, windy days.
